Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Welcome to MathSpark Kids. We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to protecting personal information while providing a safe and enjoyable mathematics learning experience for children.

1. About MathSpark Kids

MathSpark Kids is an educational mathematics application designed for children under 12. The app provides age-based lessons, quizzes, challenges, rewards, and interactive learning activities.

2. Information We May Process

When users access MathSpark Kids through the Pi ecosystem, certain information may be processed to enable authentication and app functionality. This may include a Pi username, user identifier, and information required to verify access or transactions.

MathSpark Kids does not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as a child's home address, telephone number, school information, or precise location.

3. Children's Privacy

MathSpark Kids is designed with children and families in mind. We aim to collect only information necessary to operate the application, provide learning features, maintain user access, and support legitimate transactions.

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of the application and any account or payment-related activity.

4. Pi Network Authentication

MathSpark Kids may use Pi Network authentication to identify users and provide access to application features. Authentication information is used only for operating and securing the service.

5. Payments

MathSpark Kids may offer premium access through Pi Network payments. Transaction-related information may be processed to verify successful payments and unlock eligible premium content.

MathSpark Kids does not request or store users' Pi Wallet passphrases or private keys.

6. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate MathSpark Kids.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Verify premium access and transactions.
  • Maintain app functionality and security.
  • Improve the learning experience.
  • Prevent misuse or fraudulent activity.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by services necessary to operate MathSpark Kids, including infrastructure or platform services used by the application.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with MathSpark Kids. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Services

MathSpark Kids may interact with third-party services such as Pi Network and hosting or infrastructure providers. Their handling of information may also be governed by their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MathSpark Kids introduces new features, services, or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or MathSpark Kids, please contact the MathSpark Kids developer through the official application support channel.
